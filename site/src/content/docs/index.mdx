    <h2 class="hs-section-title">How it works</h2>
    <p class="hs-section-sub">Three primitives: triggers, ACK, and UUID. No consensus algorithm. No Raft. No coordinator.</p>
    <div class="hs-flow">
      <div class="hs-flow-step">
        <div class="hs-flow-num">1</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-title">App Write</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-desc">Native SQLite speed</div>
      </div>
      <div class="hs-flow-arrow">→</div>
      <div class="hs-flow-step">
        <div class="hs-flow-num">2</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-title">Trigger</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-desc">Capture to _changes</div>
      </div>
      <div class="hs-flow-arrow">→</div>
      <div class="hs-flow-step">
        <div class="hs-flow-num">3</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-title">Ship</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-desc">HTTP POST to peers</div>
      </div>
      <div class="hs-flow-arrow">→</div>
      <div class="hs-flow-step">
        <div class="hs-flow-num">4</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-title">Apply</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-desc">INSERT OR REPLACE</div>
      </div>
      <div class="hs-flow-arrow">→</div>
      <div class="hs-flow-step">
        <div class="hs-flow-num">5</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-title">ACK</div>
        <div class="hs-flow-desc">Delete from _changes</div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="hs-section">
    <h2 class="hs-section-title">Why hook-sync?</h2>
    <p class="hs-section-sub">SQLite simplicity + multi-writer replication + cross-runtime interop, without consensus overhead.</p>
    <div class="hs-feature-grid">

      <div class="hs-feature">
        <div class="hs-feature-icon">
          <svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M4 12h16M8 8l-4 4 4 4M16 8l4 4-4 4"/></svg>
        </div>
        <h3>Multi-Writer</h3>
        <p>All nodes accept writes independently. UUID PKs = zero conflict, no coordinator, no CRDT.</p>
      </div>

      <div class="hs-feature">
        <div class="hs-feature-icon">
          <svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2"><circle cx="6" cy="12" r="3"/><circle cx="18" cy="12" r="3"/><circle cx="12" cy="6" r="3"/><line x1="8.5" y1="11" x2="10.5" y2="8" stroke-linecap="round"/><line x1="15.5" y1="11" x2="13.5" y2="8" stroke-linecap="round"/><line x1="9" y1="12" x2="15" y2="12" stroke-linecap="round"/></svg>
        </div>
        <h3>Cross-Runtime</h3>
        <p>Go, Bun, and Node sync to each other. Same wire protocol. Mix and match freely.</p>
      </div>

      <div class="hs-feature">
        <div class="hs-feature-icon">
          <svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M13 2L3 14h9l-1 8 10-12h-9l1-8z"/></svg>
        </div>
        <h3>Zero Write Penalty</h3>
        <p>Sync runs in background. Write speed identical with or without peers. 0% overhead.</p>
      </div>

        <div class="hs-feature-icon">
